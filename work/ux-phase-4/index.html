MISSION EL GHEET · UX Phase 4

Voice-first labs, one per page

Each lab is one app page with all its states, a farmer-utterance simulator, problem injection (silence, not understood, mic denied, no sound), Arabic/English, phone width and optional real browser voice. Everything is simulated: no live recognition, planning, forecasting or saving. Data is the fictional UH-001 (Uncle Hussein) fixture. Plan and research: docs/design/voice-first.md.

Flow: 01 Welcome → 02 Choose crops per season → 03 What must stay → 04 Promise Test → 05 Alternatives (optional: 06 Past weather, 07 Next 7 days) → 08 Save & takeaway. Lab 00 is the shared voice dock used on every page.

  1. 00 · Voice controller & statesPush-to-talk dock, every voice state in words, barge-in, two-miss fallback to pictures.
  2. 01 · Welcome & first useLogo + Start (audio unlock), two picture choices (check my plan / plan from the start), practice turn.
  3. 02 · Choose crops per seasonClickable four-season strip on top, a few words + pictures per question, purpose only when a crop has more than one, clarify “ذرة”, never assume a year.
  4. 03 · What must stayOne screen: lock what must not change, field facts as pictures (unknown = dashed “?”), one explicit confirm.
  5. 04 · Promise Test resultThe conflict as two pictures (maize ✕ before berseem 🔒), Why? as four steps, Other dates / Save.
  6. 05 · Other dates & alternativesNo sure alternative (UH-001), two date cards to check, before/after strip, “check again with this date?”, ≤2 alternatives layout.
  7. 06 · Past weatherOne picture per screen: offer, not available (UH-001), heat and air-dryness meters (not connected).
  8. 07 · Next 7 daysPicture + few words for not-for-this-plan / unavailable / too old; seven day tiles when available (layout only).
  9. 08 · Save & summaryCalm summary: four crops, the one problem, one Save button; full summary spoken.
