## How to use the ledger

- **Verified** means a recommendation, cultivar description or method was checked at the cited primary source within its stated scope. It can support an advisory check. A verified statement can still lack the field-specific evidence required for eligibility.
- **Observed** means a reported experimental observation or protocol. Its statement can be stored as API `evidence_status: verified` with `origin: published_study` and the trial scope. Its transfer to a new date, field or cultivar is a separate assertion requiring support.
- **Assumed** means a declared fixture fact or proposed mapping. Use API `proposed` with its actual origin, including `illustrative` where applicable. Preserve farmer confirmation independently.
- **Insufficient Evidence** maps to API `open`. A required planning check stays `unknown` until its gate is resolved. Neither a documented preference nor an observed plot date creates a hard agricultural deadline.

Cite the ledger ID and exact locator in the versioned source manifest. Capture publisher, version, access date, hash where available, units, scope and terms status per `DATA_SPEC.md`. These IDs are proposed manifest keys. No archive or study data release was created by this audit.

## Evidence gates for a real feasible option

1. **Scope and seed.** Establish matching locality, land class, grain/forage purpose, cultivar/type and availability for each season. Get the applicable wheat cultivar map and obtain current local grain-sorghum support. A dated product listing establishes a description, and seed access remains field-specific.
2. **Sowing through harvest.** Validate the actual dates or a scoped, source-supported mapping for each profile. Obtain an applicable SC10 or explicitly permitted replacement case at the candidate sowing date. Record the harvest criterion, uncertainty and distinct crop end. For berseem, establish whether and when the retained cut is practicable under the actual sowing, regrowth and irrigation schedule. Capture confirmed cut intentions without promising biomass.
3. **Release and preparation.** Record first free field after the actual final cut/harvest, removal and relevant access restrictions. Document the turnaround after release for each of the three transitions. A study interval between two plantings/harvests cannot identify the individual operations. No numeric Sakha-wide default was verified.
4. **Field suitability and commitments.** Determine the field’s relevant salinity, drainage, water table and soil/workability using compatible measurement or scoped evidence. Confirm irrigation mode, reliable access/timing and applicable restrictions. Test supported limits only. Document a forage/grain quantity check only where the farmer makes it a hard requirement and separate evidence can establish it. Crop presence cannot prove supply.
5. **Succession and optional metrics.** No hard predecessor/successor prohibition for these four crops was verified in the inspected sources. Aiad W3, Sugita M4 and Khaffagy S1 support specific observed sequences. Family repetition, legume presence, roots and crop-free gaps are advisory where sourced. A rule about disease, nitrogen credit, extra-cut water or yield requires separate evidence. Local Kc, stage boundaries and crop-specific heat thresholds are additional replay gates when those outputs are requested. [FAO56 Chapter 6, Tables 11–12](https://www.fao.org/4/x0490e/x0490e0b.htm).
