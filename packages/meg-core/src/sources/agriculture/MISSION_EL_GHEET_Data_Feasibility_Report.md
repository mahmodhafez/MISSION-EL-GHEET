## The station audit is blocked by provenance

The paper accompanying a public Egyptian weather archive attributes daily observations to the Central Laboratory of Agricultural Climate. In the downloadable archive, three files labelled Sakha give **33.300° N, 84.300° W, 300 m**. The fourth gives **31.500° N, 30.560° E, 20 m**. The 2011 file also has day-of-year codes **366–369**. Request the station log, corrected headers and dates from the authors or ARC/CLAC before comparing the data. The nearest Egyptian GHCN-Daily entry in the inspected list is Alexandria International, about **95.6 km** away. Other NOAA archives have closer stations with different coverage. No independent bias estimate is defensible yet. [Egyptian station data paper](https://odjar.org/article/download/16318/17326/19146) · [Publisher’s station archive](https://odjar.org/article/download/16318/17327) · [GHCN-D station coordinates](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/pub/data/ghcn/daily/ghcnd-stations.txt) · [NOAA station histories](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/pub/data/noaa/isd-history.csv)

**Refaat’s next two research actions** are to request corrected daily records and their observing-day metadata from ARC/CLAC, and to put the four candidate crop schedules in front of a local agronomist with Yahya. Berseem’s last cut and the first day the field is free need separate answers.
