## 3. FAO-56 daily reference demand

**Decision · conditionally feasible.** The six primary meteorological inputs are available at the reference point. The daily FAO-56 grass-reference calculation also requires a date, latitude, elevation, derived radiation and vapour-pressure terms. For daily conditions use grass albedo 0.23 and soil heat flux G ≈ 0 MJ/m²/day. The reference surface has height 0.12 m and surface resistance 70 s/m. The result has units of mm/day. [FAO-56 ch. 3](https://www.fao.org/4/x0490e/x0490e07.htm) · [FAO-56 ch. 4](https://www.fao.org/4/x0490e/x0490e08.htm)

| Quantity | Preferred method and check |
|---|---|
| T, es, Δ | Compute T = (Tmax + Tmin)/2. Let e°(T) = 0.6108 exp[17.27 T/(T+237.3)] kPa. Use es = [e°(Tmax) + e°(Tmin)]/2 and Δ = 4098 e°(T)/(T+237.3)². Keep the reported T2M as a separate variable. |
| ea and vapour-pressure deficit | Use ea ≈ e°(daily dew point), documenting the daily-mean approximation. When only mean RH is valid, FAO Eq. 19 allows ea ≈ (RHmean/100) × es. Record the substitution. Do not treat daily mean RH as RHmin or RHmax. Flag negative es − ea for review. |
| Wind and pressure | Use WS2M as u₂ and PS in kPa for γ = 0.000665 P. An alternative wind height would require FAO Eq. 47 and height metadata. Avoid a second pressure correction if NASA already adjusted for requested elevation. |
| Ra, Rso and Rs | Derive extraterrestrial radiation Ra from latitude in radians and day of year. Use Rso = (0.75 + 0.00002 z) Ra, with z in metres. In the sampled AG response, Rs already has the required unit. A different verified kWh/m²/day field needs ×3.6, and a full-day mean W/m² field needs ×0.0864. |
| Rns, Rnl and Rn | Compute Rns = 0.77 Rs. For Rnl use FAO Eq. 39, Tmax and Tmin in kelvin, ea in kPa, and the Rs/Rso cloudiness term with the FAO upper limit of 1. Check physically suspect ratios and denominator before evaluating. Rn = Rns − Rnl. |
| Daily ET₀ | Apply FAO Eq. 6 with denominator Δ + γ(1 + 0.34u₂) and numerator 0.408Δ(Rn − G) + γ[900/(T + 273)]u₂(es − ea). Required input gaps produce an unavailable result unless an explicitly approved alternative method applies. |

**Acceptance target for Mahmoud.** Chapter 4 Example 18, Uccle, 6 July, gives 3.88 mm/day before display rounding and **3.9 mm/day** to one decimal. Reproduce the example’s own humidity and radiation pathways. Compare intermediate ea, Rn and the radiation/aerodynamic contributions as well as the rounded result. Test the Sakha dew-point pathway separately against Chapter 3 identities. A formula check alone provides no local bias estimate. ## 6. Four-season replay and the fair policy test

1. Define each candidate as four linked crop slots, with exact occupancy, preparation gaps, locks and actual crop purpose. Use real consecutive dates including leap days. Show up to ten complete exploratory sequences where available, with overlap labelled.
2. Freeze the locality, eligible candidates, resource restrictions and tie-breaking rules. Both policies receive the same crops, cuts, dates, soil, irrigation and preparation rules. The calendar policy uses earliest feasible dates and a fixed minimal-change order. Only the NASA-informed policy ranks eligible plans on earlier weather. Also compare timing variants within the same sequence.
3. From valid development Tmax estimate the local 90th percentile and freeze its numeric value, fitting years and quantile convention. For valid days count Tmax > threshold, report fraction of valid days and sum max(Tmax − threshold, 0) in °C·days. For demand show ET₀ sum (mm), mean per valid day (mm/day) and duration. Preserve losses, ties and ranking reversals.
4. Advance forecast origins. Exclude development windows crossing the origin and evaluate the next complete, non-overlapping four-season block. Reserve the last eligible block until the comparison protocol is frozen. Keep chronology continuous across winter years.
5. Use the same weather block and input-specific valid-date mask across policies. Retain each plan’s own crop windows. A conservative proposed primary rule is to exclude blocks with a missing required day anywhere in the compared windows for that metric. Report exclusions and matched coverage.
